### 🌐 English Summary:

The Additional Solicitor General informed the court today (25) that there was a close relationship between former Director of the State Intelligence Service Suresh Saleh and Azad Maulana.

Accordingly, Additional Solicitor General Dileepa Peiris has stated this while making a lengthy statement in this regard.Also, presenting evidence to the court, Additional Solicitor General Dileepa Peiris stated that the statements made by two of the defendants on the day the verdict in the main case of this incident was delivered were investigated.

The Additional Solicitor General stated this in his statement to the court regarding this matter.

"Your Honor, the verdict in the major case related to this case was given by the Colombo Three-Judge High Court yesterday. Two of the defendants had stated in their pre-sentence statements that other individuals were involved in this incident. Accordingly, the Criminal Investigation Department officers had gone to the prison to obtain statements from them. However, the defendants had not given statements at that time. They had said that they would discuss with their families and give statements. The reason they had given was that if they gave such statements, their families' lives could be threatened. The first witness in the Great Easter case is former Intelligence Chief Suresh Saleh and the second witness is Rohan Gunaratne. This also makes it clear that this was planned and done. Furthermore, this third suspect has given statements that he had no relationship with Azad Maulana. However, the investigations conducted revealed that Azad Maulana and Suresh Saleh had a close relationship. It is proven, Sir. Ten members of the family, including Suresh Sale, have spent one lakh thirty thousand nine hundred and forty-four rupees and taken four rooms in a hotel near Ahungalla.
