තුවාල ලැබූ පුද්ගලයින් රත්නපුර ශික්ෂණ රෝහලට ඇතුළත් කර ඇති බව පොලීසිය වැඩිදුරටත් සඳහන් කර සිටියා.

### 🌐 English Summary:

16 people have been injured in an accident involving a private bus and a tipper truck in the Hidellana area of Ratnapura.

It is stated that the accident occurred when a private bus traveling from Avissawella to Ratnapura and a tipper truck traveling from Ratnapura to Avissawella collided head-on.

The police further stated that the injured individuals have been admitted to the Ratnapura Teaching Hospital.
